- Solution: `3x + 9 = 3x + k` → `9 = k`
- Known mistakes: Both sides need the same constant: +9 on the left means k = +9, not −9. · The x terms already match (3x on both sides). Only the constants must match: k = 9. · The 3x terms cancel and are not added to the constant: k = 9.

> For infinitely many solutions, both sides must end up identical. Subtract three x from both sides: nine equals k. So k must be nine, and then both sides are three x plus nine.

**Objective.** Show, step by step, why the answer to `50 + 6v = 10v` is `v = (25)/(2)`, and name the mistakes that lead to the wrong answers. Level 4 (Boss level), solve the equation.

**Topics.**
- Solution: `50 + 6v = 10v` → `50 = 4v` → `v = (25)/(2)`
- Known mistakes: The v terms are on opposite sides: 10v − 6v = 4v. Adding gives 16v. · To undo 4v you divide by 4; subtracting 4 from 50 doesn't undo it. · 50 = 4v means v = 50 ÷ 4, not 4 ÷ 50.

#### S01 · The problem
*Screen:* `50 + 6v = 10v` (show-problem)
> Let's solve this one step by step. Gym A charges fifty dollars to join plus six dollars a visit. Gym B charges ten dollars a visit. After how many visits do they cost the same? Let v be the number of visits, so fifty plus six v equals ten v.

#### S02 · Step by step
*Screen:* `50 = 4v` (step-1)
> Subtract six v from both sides. Fifty equals four v.
*Screen:* `v = (25) over (2)` (step-2)
> Divide both sides by four. v equals twenty-five over two, which is twelve and a half. Visits come in whole numbers, so the costs cross between visit twelve and visit thirteen.
#### S03 · The answer
*Screen:* `v = 25/2` (answer)
> So v equals twenty-five over two.
> Watch out for these traps. If you add six v instead of subtracting it, you get twenty-five over eight. If you subtract four instead of dividing, you get forty-six.
