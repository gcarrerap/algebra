# Algebra 1 Syllabus (Grade 9, Kentucky)

Syllabus for Ana (Grade 9, Ignite Institute, Kentucky), based on the
**Algebra 1 (270304)** course of the *Kentucky Academic Standards* (KAS) for
Mathematics. Each unit lists the standards it covers.

> Source: [Kentucky Academic Standards — Algebra 1](https://education.ky.gov/curriculum/standards/kyacadstand/Documents/270304_Algebra_1_2019.pdf)

---

## Unit 0 — Foundations and Expressions
*KY.HS.A.1, A.2*

1. Order of operations and signed numbers
2. Properties: commutative, associative, distributive
3. Parts of an expression: terms, factors, coefficients, constants
4. Combining like terms and simplifying
5. Translating words into algebraic expressions

## Unit 1 — Linear Equations and Inequalities
*KY.HS.A.12, A.15, A.16, A.18, N.4–N.6*

1. One-step and multi-step equations
2. Variables on both sides; no solution and infinitely many solutions
3. Justifying each step (properties of equality)
4. Linear inequalities and graphing them on a number line
5. Compound inequalities (*and / or*)
6. Literal equations: solving formulas for a variable (e.g. `A = ½bh` for `h`)
7. Problems with units and dimensional analysis

## Unit 2 — Functions
*KY.HS.F.1, A.23*

1. Relations vs. functions (tables, mapping diagrams, vertical line test)
2. Function notation `f(x)` and evaluating functions
3. Domain and range (in context and from graphs)
4. Reading and interpreting graphs: increasing, decreasing, intercepts
5. A graph as the set of all solutions of an equation

## Unit 3 — Linear Functions
*KY.HS.F.3, F.4, F.12, F.14, A.13, A.24*

1. Rate of change and slope
2. Slope-intercept form `y = mx + b`
3. Point-slope form and standard form
4. Writing equations from a graph, two points, a table, or a description
5. Parallel and perpendicular lines
6. Interpreting slope and intercept in context
7. Solving `f(x) = g(x)` with graphs and tables

## Unit 4 — Sequences
*KY.HS.F.2, F.7*

1. Arithmetic sequences: explicit and recursive formulas
2. Geometric sequences: explicit and recursive formulas
3. Sequences as functions defined on the integers
4. Modeling real situations with sequences

## Unit 5 — Systems of Equations and Inequalities
*KY.HS.A.14, A.20, A.25*

1. Solving by graphing
2. Solving by substitution
3. Solving by elimination (and why it works)
4. Number of solutions: one, none, infinitely many
5. Linear inequalities in two variables (half-planes)
6. Systems of inequalities and constraint problems

## Unit 6 — Statistics: Two-Variable Data
*KY.HS.SP.6, SP.7, SP.8*

1. Scatter plots and types of association
2. Line of best fit and residuals
3. Interpreting the slope and intercept of a model
4. Correlation coefficient `r`
5. Correlation is not causation

## Unit 7 — Exponents and Radicals
*KY.HS.N.1, N.2*

1. Exponent rules (product, quotient, power)
2. Zero and negative exponents
3. Roots and radicals; simplifying radicals
4. Rational exponents: `a^(1/n)` and `a^(m/n)`

## Unit 8 — Exponential Functions
*KY.HS.F.5, F.11, F.12, F.13, F.14*

1. Linear vs. exponential: constant differences vs. constant factors
2. Exponential growth and decay `y = a·bˣ`
3. Percent change and compound interest
4. Building exponential functions from tables and two points
5. Why exponential growth eventually beats linear and quadratic growth

## Unit 9 — Polynomials
*KY.HS.A.3, A.5*

1. Vocabulary: degree, standard form, monomial, binomial, trinomial
2. Adding and subtracting polynomials
3. Multiplying polynomials (distributive property, area model)
4. Special products: `(a+b)²`, `(a−b)²`, `(a+b)(a−b)`

## Unit 10 — Factoring
*KY.HS.A.2, A.3, A.7*

1. Greatest common factor (GCF)
2. Trinomials `x² + bx + c`
3. Trinomials `ax² + bx + c`
4. Difference of squares and perfect-square trinomials
5. Factoring to find zeros (roots)

## Unit 11 — Quadratic Functions
*KY.HS.F.4, F.5, A.7*

1. The parabola: vertex, axis of symmetry, intercepts
2. Standard form, vertex form, and factored form
3. What each form reveals (zeros, maximum/minimum, symmetry)
4. Transformations of `y = x²`
5. Quadratic models (projectiles, area)

## Unit 12 — Solving Quadratic Equations
*KY.HS.A.19*

1. Solving by factoring
2. Solving with square roots
3. Completing the square
4. The quadratic formula and the discriminant
5. Choosing the best method

## Unit 13 — Modeling and Final Review
*KY.HS.F.6, F.13, N.4*

1. Choosing a model: linear, exponential, or quadratic
2. Combining functions with operations
3. Capstone project with real data

## Lesson Format

Each lesson is a self-contained HTML page with:

1. **Key idea** — a short, plain-language explanation.
2. **Interactive visual** — an animation or manipulative (balance scale, draggable graph, area model…).
3. **Worked examples** — revealed step by step.
4. **Practice** — randomly generated problems with instant feedback.
5. **Challenge** — a problem in context or a reasoning question.

## Mathematical Practices (throughout)

Make sense of problems and persevere · reason abstractly and quantitatively ·
construct arguments and critique reasoning · model with mathematics · use tools
strategically · attend to precision · look for structure · look for regularity
in repeated reasoning.
