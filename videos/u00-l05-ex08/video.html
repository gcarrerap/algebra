Write an expression for “three more than twice a number”. Use x for the number.
Three more than twice a number

ORDER

2x + 3

Common traps
3x + 2
The three is added after the doubling; only the number is doubled.
2(x + 3)
Twice a number is just 2x; the three is added after, with no parentheses.
2x − 3
More than means add, not subtract.
