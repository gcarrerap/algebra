− and ÷ are not commutative: order matters.
Associative= grouping
(2 + 3) + 4 = 5 + 4 = 9
2 + (3 + 4) = 2 + 7 = 9
Same order. Only the parentheses move.
(a + b) + c = a + (b + c)
(a · b) · c = a · (b · c)
