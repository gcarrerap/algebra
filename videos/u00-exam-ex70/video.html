Write an expression for “two less than five times the difference of a number and three”. Use x for the number.
Two less than five times the difference of a number and three
GROUP
Two less than five times (x − 3)
WORDS
Two less than 5(x − 3)
ORDER
5(x − 3) − 2
5(x − 3) − 2
x = 7: 5(x − 3) − 2 → 18
Common traps
5x − 3 − 2
The five multiplies the whole difference, so it needs parentheses.
5(x − 3 − 2)
The two is taken away from the product, not inside the group.
5(x − 3) + 2
Less than means take away, not add.
