Solution · Translating words into expressions · Boss level
A pizza costs 12 dollars plus 2 dollars for each topping. Three friends share the cost equally. Write an expression for what each friend pays when there are x toppings.
Cost: 12 plus 2 for each of x toppings, shared by 3
WORDS
12 + 2x, shared by 3
GROUP
(12 + 2x) ÷ 3
WORDS
12 + 2x3
12 + 2x3
x = 6: 12 + 2x3 → 8
Common traps
12 + 2x3
The three friends share the whole cost, not just the toppings.
12 + 2x
You still have to share the cost among three friends.
2x3
The twelve dollars is part of the cost that gets shared.
